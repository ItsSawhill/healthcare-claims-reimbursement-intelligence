## Source Classes

### official_cms_synthetic

Patient, Coverage, and PDE EOB templates were adapted from the existing CMS Blue Button synthetic sample fixtures under:

`tests/fixtures/fhir/cms_blue_button/`

The resources were modified to create multiple synthetic beneficiary aliases, unique resource IDs, shifted service dates, varied amounts, and varied provider identifiers.

### documentation_based_fixture

Carrier and Outpatient EOB templates were adapted from local documentation-based Blue Button-style fixtures under:

`tests/fixtures/fhir/cms_blue_button/local_documentation_based_eob_bundle.json`

These resources are used to exercise professional and institutional claim shapes. They are not treated as an official downloaded CMS population sample.

## Resource Provenance Tags

Each generated resource includes `meta.tag` values for:

- provenance classification
- source dataset
- beneficiary alias

Bronze ingestion extracts these tags into:

- `provenance_classification`
- `source_dataset`
- `beneficiary_alias`
- `source_type`
- `acquisition_batch`
- `original_resource_id`

## Exclusions

No real PHI, OAuth credentials, access tokens, refresh tokens, client secrets, Databricks secrets, passwords, or live beneficiary credentials are included.

## Known Limitations

- This is an adapted synthetic engineering dataset, not a CMS production or research dataset.
- It should not be used to estimate real Medicare utilization, reimbursement, PMPM, provider performance, fraud, or anomaly rates.
- Live CMS sandbox extraction remains future work once authorized credentials are available.
